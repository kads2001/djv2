About this spirit

  • Master Blending: Conceived by master distillers blending premium grain spirits and aged spirits for supreme harmony.
  • Sensory Notes: Inviting notes of toasted American oak, rich heather honey, roasted malt, and subtle whispers of vanilla orchid and dried stone fruit.
