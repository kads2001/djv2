About this spirit

  • Botanical Distillation: Distilled in specialized copper baskets, vapor-infusing wild juniper berries, coriander seeds, angelica root, and fresh citrus rinds.
  • Sensory Notes: Crisp piney juniper upfront, followed by crushed coriander, zesty lime peel, and delicate floral elderflower hints.
  • Palate Experience: Invigorating citrus burst, smooth pepperiness, herbal elegance, and crystal-clear purity across the palate.
  • Lingering Finish: Dry, clean, and exhilarating with a vibrant citrus peel and refreshing juniper afterglow.
  • Global Export Standards: Certified under FSSAI and ISO 9001 guidelines; export cartons and tamper-proof caps standard.
